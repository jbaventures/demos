Supply Chain & Logistics

Stable supply and continuity in volatile theatres. The right part, in the right place, at the right time.

01

What we do

Supply is where sustainment fails first. Ours holds.

We manage defence supply chains from end to end, from sourcing and procurement through storage, distribution and delivery to the point of use, by land, sea and air with live tracking.

Operating as a regional supply and procurement hub, we mobilise across Europe, Africa, Asia and the Middle East. We work with OEMs and trusted suppliers to secure genuine parts and shorten lead times, so platforms spend less time waiting.

The problem we solve

Supply chains for defence platforms in operational environments are complex.

Without a stable supply chain, platforms wait for parts, readiness suffers and costs escalate.

  • 01Long lead times for critical components.
  • 02Multiple suppliers across different jurisdictions.
  • 03Export control and end-user certification requirements.
  • 04Limited in-country storage and logistics infrastructure.
  • 05Demand variability driven by operational tempo.
02

How we keep supply stable

Sourced, moved, held and cleared

What we doHow we do it
Sourcing and procurementGenuine parts from OEMs and vetted suppliers, checked for authenticity and compliance with military standards.
LogisticsInternational and in-country movement by land, sea and air, tracked from origin to destination, including last-mile delivery.
Inventory and warehousingStock held where it's needed and kept at the right levels, so supply stays steady when demand doesn't.
ComplianceDue diligence on every supplier for sanctions, politically exposed persons and adverse media. End-user certification, export licensing and cross-border agreements handled for you.
03

Proof of performance

Efficiency at scale

On the US Army's A-TEMP programme, AMS managed a $74m annual supply budget, procured and delivered more than 28,000 items, and saved $27m a year.

On NMS-GVS, we delivered 100% of supply chain services through a verified network of local and international suppliers.
