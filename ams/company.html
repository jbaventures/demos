02

How we work

Three principles

AMS operates according to three principles that guide every decision and action.

01

Impose discipline

Complex environments demand structure.

We enforce standards, maintain configuration control, and follow disciplined sustainment practices. We resist shortcuts that create future instability.

If it weakens discipline, it weakens stability.

02

Simplify complexity

Complexity is the enemy of readiness.

We remove operational friction, clarify processes and responsibilities, and bring order to fragmented environments.

If it adds complexity, it must add clear value – or it doesn't belong.

03

Execute under pressure

Operational environments rarely allow perfect conditions.

We remain calm in high-tempo situations, solve problems without creating new risks, and deliver reliably even when circumstances change.

If it won't hold under pressure, it isn't the right solution.

Our mantra

Remove complexity.
Protect readiness.

03

Where we operate

Defence platforms are deployed globally. Support must follow.

AMS delivers in-service support across two operating modes.

Mode one

Established operations

Providing structured, long-term sustainment within defined regions and facilities.

  • Permanent regional presence and workforce.
  • Scheduled maintenance and overhaul programmes.
  • Supply chain and inventory management.
  • Training and capability development.
Mode two

Expeditionary operations

Deploying specialist teams into overseas theatres to inspect, maintain and sustain platforms where they operate.

  • Forward-deployed maintenance and sustainment.
  • Technical inspection and assessment missions.
  • Operational support in contested or austere environments.
  • Rapid response to emerging capability requirements.
