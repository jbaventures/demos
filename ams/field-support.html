Field Support Representatives

A trusted expert presence where platforms operate, protecting availability, standards and your team's self-sufficiency.

01

What we do

Expertise at the point of need

FSRs provide on-site technical expertise, quality assurance, and OEM representation – ensuring platforms are maintained to standard wherever they're deployed.

They resolve technical issues where they arise, hold quality to OEM standard and give OEMs clear visibility of platform condition and usage. Performance stays stable, wherever the platform is.

  • 40+
    Countries of operation
  • Live
    FSR and technical reach-back across our Ukraine sustainment projects

The problem we solve

Platforms deployed far from OEM facilities need expert support.

Without FSR presence, problems escalate, standards slip and platforms underperform.

  • 01Local maintainers may lack platform-specific knowledge.
  • 02Quality standards can erode without oversight.
  • 03Technical issues require rapid, authoritative resolution.
  • 04OEMs need visibility of platform condition and usage.
